"Each building has stood since the 18th century. We simply made them worth staying in."

Residentas was born from a belief that the best way to experience Lisbon is to live in it. Not to sleep in a hotel corridor, but to wake up in a Pombaline apartment, hear the trams on the cobblestones, and walk to breakfast through streets that haven't changed in three centuries.

About Residentas

Apartments built
for real stays.

Every Residentas apartment is designed around one question: what does someone actually need for a week in Lisbon? The answer isn't a minibar and a turndown service. It's a proper kitchen, a fast connection, blackout curtains, and a bed you don't want to leave. Space to work. Room to breathe. A neighbourhood outside your door instead of a lobby.

Neighbourhood

Bairro Alto — Lisbon's creative soul.

Bairro Alto has been Lisbon's bohemian heart since the 16th century. Its grid of narrow streets hides some of the city's best restaurants, fado houses, art galleries and independent shops — all within a few minutes' walk.

By day it's quiet and residential; by evening it transforms into one of Lisbon's most vibrant neighbourhoods. From São Pedro you're also steps from Chiado and 10 minutes from Príncipe Real.
